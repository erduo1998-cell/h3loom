# Motion study and implementation notes

Reference supplied by the owner: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3uAIqqg8ZHo
Examined in the browser, paused and advanced in 8-frame increments around the
opening, 5–6 seconds, 50–51 seconds and 65–66 seconds. Times are approximate UI
readings, not an extracted edit decision list. Other contextual frames were
examined at 10, 20, 35 and 70 seconds. No reference footage is included.

| Observation | Consequence for the hand-coded film |
| --- | --- |
| The opening product approaches gently, then rapidly grows and rotates; highlight position changes with orientation. | Separate the calm approach from the acceleration; animate a real beveled object under independent moving softboxes. |
| Giant WITH cuts to the rear product view; the next huge model name shrinks into the identifier on a chip. | Alternate type and objects. Reuse the established object inside later storyboard frames; connect large type to small physical labels by scale. |
| RENDER stays almost stationary while blue, violet, pink and warm reflections flow over the letters. | Animate the material field separately from type. Keep a black background, deep blue shadow and limited warm highlights. |
| A wireframe object becomes solid from bottom to top immediately after RENDER. | A change in visual representation should explain progress, not simply decorate a cut. Do not claim that an arbitrary line wipe reproduces this effect. |
| ONE MORE TIME mixes a very wide ONE with tall MORE, includes the product as a cell, then is pushed down by dense specification text. | Compose each word to its own rectangle. Use changing cell proportions and a motivated displacement, not a repeated centered-title preset. |

## Current 24-second timeline

- 0–2.60: cloud module approach, accelerated rotation, grazing edge reflection.
- 2.60–3.35: SRT fills the frame, restrained horizontal expansion.
- 3.35–4.60: DESIGN changes aspect ratio, settles, rapidly compresses.
- 4.60–7.40: four views of the same module fan into storyboard cells; camera
  travels into the upper-right cell.
- 7.40–8.45: RENDER holds while cobalt/warm reflectance travels across its face.
- 8.45–12.40: tilted cloud die resolves toward the camera; a vertical material
  reveal turns its blueprint into a solid surface, then the camera pushes through.
- 12.40–14.20: 4K arrives oversized and settles; the interference field continues.
- 14.20–17.00: asymmetric FROM SRT / TO 4K composition resolves and exits downward.
- 17.00–20.10: return to the established module with a different camera distance.
- 20.10–24.00: H3LOOM signature and project description.

The model is an original visual metaphor for the cloud runtime, not a physical
H3Loom hardware product. Storyboard images are locally rendered from this model,
not H3 output samples. The typography uses installed OS fonts, not Apple artwork.

## Review criteria

Review the encoded movie, not only a contact sheet: check word readability during
holds, acceleration before each transition, position continuity within a shot,
reflection movement independent of object movement, black levels and highlight
clipping, storyboard-cell order, audio accents at cuts, and the final title hold.
This study records observed mechanisms; it does not assert a frame-exact remake
or equivalence to Apple's production quality.
